# Monetisation — Experiments, not promises

## Keep the first pilot free
Early usage and student feedback are more valuable than charging before the product is useful.

## Possible premium tier (future)
- Multiple semester/course planners
- Recurring tasks and templates
- Calendar sync/export
- Weekly planning suggestions
- Advanced progress summaries
- Personalised routines

## Pricing experiment
Once the features exist, test a few price points with interviews or a waitlist. Avoid making a paid offer before users understand the value. South African student affordability should be researched.

## Other routes
- University wellness/academic-support partnerships
- Sponsored campus pilots
- Institutional licences, if procurement and privacy requirements can be met

## Avoid for now
- Paid ads
- Expensive app development
- Collecting health data unnecessarily
- Claims that the app treats stress or improves grades
