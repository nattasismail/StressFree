# StressFree Privacy Notice — Draft

**This is a starting draft, not legal advice. Review and adapt before publishing.**

StressFree is an early student-planning prototype. The current prototype stores tasks and optional check-in notes in local storage in the user's browser/device. It does not currently create an account or send this data to a StressFree server.

## Data in the current prototype
- Tasks and due dates you enter
- Task completion status and priorities
- Focus sessions completed
- Optional daily stress score and notes

## Storage and control
Information is stored in the browser/device used. Clearing browser data or changing devices may erase or make it unavailable. Do not enter highly sensitive personal information.

## Future versions
If StressFree adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, payment, or support services, this notice must be updated to explain what is collected, why, legal basis, retention, processors, security, user rights, and contact details.
