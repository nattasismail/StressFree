# StressFree Usability Pilot

## Participant briefing
StressFree is a prototype. We are testing the product, not you. Please do not enter private or sensitive details. You may stop at any time.

## Tasks
1. Add a task with a due date and high priority.
2. Find that task on the dashboard.
3. Mark it complete and undo/review.
4. Start and reset a focus timer.
5. Submit an optional stress check-in.
6. Explain what you would expect StressFree to do next.

## Questions
- What was easiest?
- What was confusing?
- What felt unnecessary?
- What feature would make you use it weekly?
- Would you use it again next week? Why?
- Would you pay for any feature? Which, and what price range feels reasonable?
- What privacy concerns do you have?

## Record
Avoid recording identifying or sensitive details unless necessary and with informed consent. Report aggregate themes.
